# Blocky Obby

A Minecraft-style obby (obstacle course) generator that runs in your browser — with chaotic tweenslop animations, emotes, and online multiplayer.

▶️ **Play it here: https://rc7912.github.io/blocky-obby/**

## Features

- **Endless courses** — every seed makes a different obby. Pick the number of stages and the difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard).
- **Obstacles** — jumps, thin beams, moving platforms, spinners, slime bounce pads, zigzags, disappearing glass, lava floors, magma strips, conveyor belts, slippery ice and sweeping magma pillars, plus:
  - 🚀 **Launch pads** that fling you across a huge gap onto the next platform
  - 🪨 **Falling gravel** that shakes and drops a moment after you land
  - 🌬️ **Wind zones** where a fan blows you toward the edge in gusts
  - 🪓 **Swinging pendulums** with axe blades sweeping across the path
  - 🧱 **Wall jumps** — jump back and forth between two tall walls to climb to a ledge
  - 🪜 **Ladders and vines** up tall pillars (just walk into them to climb)
  - 🌀 **Portals** that teleport you across gaps far too wide to jump
  - 🔓 **Levers** that make hidden glass stepping stones appear for a few seconds
- **Five worlds** — Overworld, Nether, Frozen Peaks, The End and 🪟 Windows XP (Bliss sky and hills, explorer-window and taskbar platforms, Start-button checkpoints, and its own startup chime), each with its own blocks, sky and floating particles. Pick one or let the seed choose.
- **Power-ups** — ⚡ speed boost, 🦘 super jump (with a triple jump) and 🛡️ a shield that saves you from one lava hit.
- **Coins and a hat shop** — grab 🪙 along the course; they're banked when you finish, plus a finish bonus. Spend them on hats like a crown, top hat, halo or a spinning propeller cap.
- **Pets** — adopt a 🐶 dog, 🐱 cat, 🦆 duck, 🟩 slime, 💚 creeper, 🐝 bee or 🐉 dragon in the 🛒 shop. Walkers hop along behind you, the bee and dragon fly with flapping wings, and other players see your pet too.
- **Badges** — 16 badges to earn, like 🏁 First Finish, 🔥 Hardcore, 😇 Untouchable (no deaths), ⚡ Speedrunner, 🌍 World Traveler, 🥇 Champion, 🦉 Night Owl, 💀 Professional Dier and 💥 Kaboom. Open **🏅 Badges** to see your progress and pick one to show next to your name and in chat for other players.
- **Best times and ghosts** — your best time per course is saved, and a see-through 👻 ghost replays your best run so you can race yourself.
- **Daily challenge** — one course per day that's the same for everyone, so it's a good place to meet other players.
- **Realistic textures** — the Overworld, Nether, Frozen Peaks and The End use detailed textures with bumpy surfaces (grass blades, pebbly dirt, cobblestone, wood grain, glowing magma cracks, flowing lava). Obstacles get rounder too: spinning bars become glowing hot rods, sliding pillars and poles become round columns, power-ups are glass orbs and coins are stamped gold. Clouds become soft and fluffy and the sun is round. Turn off **🌄 Realistic textures** in ⚙️ Settings to go back to the blocky pixel look.
- **Minecraft look** — pixel textures for grass, dirt, sand, snow, ice, stone, bricks, planks, netherrack, end stone, purpur, obsidian, XP windows, gold, emerald and diamond, plus blocky clouds.
- **Double jump** — press jump again in the air.
- **Skins** — 19 characters. Ten come with their own body: 📟 **Microwave** (hums and lights up while you stand still, then beeps when you walk off), 💀 **Skeleton** (bones rattle when you jump), 🚽 **Toilet Head** (a grinning head in a toilet that flushes when you explode), 🦆 **Rubber Duck** (squeaks when you jump), 🟣 **Enderman** (tall and thin with glowing purple eyes and purple particles), 💚 **Creeper** (hisses when you explode), 📎 **Paperclip Helper** with googly eyes, 🧑‍🚀 **Crewmate**, 🍌 dancing **Banana** and a big green 👹 **Swamp Ogre**. Or make your own with custom colors, trails and neon glow (the Shirt color also recolors the Crewmate).
- **Character sounds** — every character has its own jump sound and an idle sound it makes every few seconds while you stand still: the Ninja swishes, Gold King jingles coins, Inferno crackles, the Enderman goes *vwoop*, the Rubber Duck squeaks and quacks, the Swamp Ogre burps, the Banana slide-whistles, the Toilet drips and splashes, and more.
- **Two art styles** — switch between blocky Roblox-style and a round paper-cutout South Park style in the menu. Your skin colors work with both, and other players see the style you picked.
- **Tweenslop animation** — squash and stretch, flips, bobble head, screen shake and meme popups.
- **Emotes** — dance, wave, dab, floss, sit, cheer, breakdance, laugh, play dead… or explode 💥.
- **Multiplayer** — everyone on the same course plays together, with name tags and chat. Press **🏁 Race** for a countdown race where everyone restarts together and finishers are ranked 🥇🥈🥉.
- **Settings** — volume, music, look sensitivity, field of view, and toggles for shadows, screen shake, meme popups, speed lines, the ghost, realistic textures and day & night.
- **A song for every world** — original music made in code (so it's copyright-free): a bouncy adventure tune in the Overworld, war drums and a growling bass in the Nether, a sparkly music box in Frozen Peaks, spacey synthwave in The End and easy-listening lounge in Windows XP. The music goes quiet when you stand still, and your character's dance follows each song's beat.
- **Day and night** — the Overworld, Frozen Peaks and Windows XP cycle through sunrise, day, an orange sunset and a starry night with a moon (fireflies come out in the Overworld). A full day takes 5 minutes, and everyone online shares the same time of day. Turn it off with **🌗 Day & night** in ⚙️ Settings.
- **AFK sleeping** — leave the game alone for 20 seconds and your character lies down on the floor and snores 💤. Press any key to wake up.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Jump (press again in the air to double jump) |
| Mouse (click the game first) | Look around |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom |
| Q / E | Turn the camera |
| 1 – 9 | Emotes |
| X | Explode 💥 |
| T or Enter | Chat |
| R | Respawn at checkpoint |
| M | Music on/off |

On phones: drag the left side of the screen to move, drag the right side to look, and tap **JUMP**.

## Playing with friends

Multiplayer joins everyone who is on the **same course** (same seed, stages and difficulty).

1. Make a course you like.
2. Click **Copy link** and send it to your friends.
3. When they open it, you'll see them in the game and the top right shows how many people are online.

### Servers

Click **🖥️ Servers** (in the menu or on the title screen) to see every server that has players right now: its name, how many people are on it (up to 25), and its course. Click **Join** to hop in.

To make your own, set up a course in the menu, type a name under **New server** and click **Create & join**. **Copy link** also shares the server, so friends land in the same one. Everyone starts on the **Main** server for their course, and making a new course takes you back to Main.

Want to play solo? Click **🌐 Multiplayer on** in the menu to switch to **📴 Offline mode** — you won't connect to anyone, and the game remembers your choice. Click it again to go back online.

Players connect directly to each other (WebRTC peer-to-peer via [Trystero](https://github.com/dmotz/trystero)); public relays are only used to help players find each other. Don't share personal info in chat — anyone on the same course can see it.

## Running it yourself

It's a single HTML file with no build step. Download the repo and serve the folder with any static web server, for example:

```bash
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.
